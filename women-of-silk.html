The Women of Silk.

Real women. Real stories. This is the flagship — the franchise every other format in Built in Silk grows out of.

Fame isn't the qualification

The Women of Silk isn't a highlight reel of the already-arrived. Fame isn't the qualification here — the story, the perspective, the honesty and the emotional truth are what make someone interesting.

We're looking for the women mid-build: the ones whose stories are still unfolding, and who are willing to tell them plainly.

What are you becoming now? What do you wish another woman knew?

Share your story
"Real women. Real stories. Flagship human storytelling."

What we're looking for

No résumé required. If any of this sounds like you, we want to hear from you.

  • You're building something — a career, a business, a creative practice, a version of yourself — and it isn't finished yet
  • You have a real relationship with ambition, money, identity or reinvention, messy parts included
  • You're willing to be honest about what the becoming has actually cost, and what it's given you
  • You have a perspective, not just a highlight reel
