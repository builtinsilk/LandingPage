You can be ambitious

Built in Silk is not a manual for becoming a particular kind of woman. It's an editorial space for women thinking about who they are, what they want, and what they are building.

What we're building →
Built

Work. Ambition. Discipline. Wealth. Growth. The life being constructed.

+
Silk

Softness. Femininity. Elegance. Beauty. Texture. Ease.

Together, the name holds the tension that makes this brand distinctive: a woman can be powerful and soft at the same time.

Who this is for

  • Women building careers, businesses, creative lives or financial independence
  • Women navigating identity, adulthood, relationships, friendship and reinvention
  • Women who enjoy beauty and elegance but want substance behind the aesthetic
  • Women who are ambitious without wanting to perform a hard, hyper-competitive version of success
  • Women who are still becoming, and don't feel represented by perfect, finished success stories

Who it isn't for

  • A generic "girlboss" account
  • A prescriptive femininity manual
  • A luxury shopping catalogue
  • A motivational quote page
  • A publication that sacrifices substance for aesthetics
